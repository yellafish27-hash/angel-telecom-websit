Repair or replace a business phone? A practical cost guide

Replacing a damaged phone is not always the most economical decision.

A cracked screen or ageing battery may be repairable, allowing the business to keep a suitable device in service. But reliability, security support, downtime and repair cost all matter.

Compare total cost

Compare a realistic repair quotation with replacement cost, setup time and potential downtime.

Consider device security

A phone that no longer receives appropriate security updates may need replacement even if it still works.

Battery and screen repairs

Battery replacement or screen repair can extend useful life when the underlying device remains suitable.

Consider refurbished devices

A quality refurbished handset may offer better value than buying new, depending on business requirements.
