GAMES CONSOLE REPAIRS

Common faults we can help with.

Pocket Geek Tech Repair supports major gaming platforms including PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo Switch.

HDMI & no picture

No signal, intermittent picture or a damaged HDMI port can prevent a console from displaying properly.

Overheating & loud fans

Dust, restricted airflow and cooling problems can cause excessive fan noise or shutdowns.

Stick drift & TMR upgrades

Controllers or Joy-Cons moving without being touched can be caused by worn analogue sticks. Where supported, we can upgrade standard analogue sticks to TMR (Tunnel Magnetoresistance) magnetic technology for a more durable controller upgrade.

Disc drive faults

Problems reading, accepting or ejecting discs can be caused by the drive mechanism or laser.

Power faults

We can diagnose consoles that won't turn on, lose power unexpectedly or show other power-related symptoms.

Storage & software issues

Crashes, failed updates, installation problems and storage faults can sometimes be diagnosed and resolved.

CONTROLLER UPGRADES

Upgrade analogue sticks with Hall Effect or TMR technology.

For compatible controllers, Pocket Geek Tech Repair can replace traditional analogue stick modules with modern magnetic sensing technology. Hall Effect and TMR designs reduce the physical wear associated with conventional potentiometer sticks and can help address stick-drift problems.

Hall EffectMagnetic sensing with no traditional resistive track.
TMRAdvanced magnetic sensing technology for supported controllers.
Compatible modelsAvailability depends on the controller and replacement parts available.
