BUSINESS MOBILE OPTIONS

Choose the setup that fits your business.

Different sized businesses have different priorities. The cheapest deal is not always the cheapest solution once support, downtime, administration and avoidable mobile spend are taken into account.

01

Small business

1–9 connections

Often a few owners, family members, sales people or drivers. There are cheaper deals available, but these can come with little or no personal support. If all you need is a bill and nothing ever goes wrong, that can work.

But when something does go wrong (a phone is lost, a SIM stops working, someone is abroad or a bill looks wrong), having a real person you can speak to can make all the difference.

Discuss your requirements →
02

Growing business

10–49 connections

Once you have dozens of devices, mobiles can become another job for someone in the business. Billing queries, stolen or broken handsets, new starters, leavers, renewals and upgrades all take time.

You increasingly need a supplier who understands your business, knows how to resolve problems quickly and can help prevent them happening in the first place. A dedicated point of contact, backed by an online portal where appropriate, can take a lot of the day-to-day administration away from your team.

Discuss your requirements →
03

Larger business

50+ connections

The same pressures as a growing business, but multiplied across a much larger user estate. Small billing, tariff or usage issues can become significant costs when they are repeated across dozens or hundreds of connections.

At this level, fast self-service becomes increasingly important. 24/7 access to a dedicated portal can allow authorised users to make handset changes, arrange SIM swaps, add bolt-ons or bundles and block SIMs when needed, while your dedicated contact remains there for the issues that need a person.
