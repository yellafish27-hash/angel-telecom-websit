PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

How Angel Telecom handles information submitted through this website and how you can contact us about privacy.

Information you choose to send us

If you use our online enquiry form, we may receive your name, company, email address, enquiry details and any file you choose to attach. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and provide the service you have requested.

How we use enquiry information

Information submitted through the website is used for legitimate business purposes connected with your enquiry, such as responding to questions, preparing quotations, discussing mobile or technology requirements, or arranging repair and support services.

Files and mobile bills

If you attach a document, such as a mobile bill or device list, please only include information that is relevant to your enquiry. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information unless we specifically ask for it through an appropriate secure process.

Third-party services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Online enquiry forms are processed by FormSubmit, which forwards submitted information to Angel Telecom. The free mobile bill review upload facility uses Microsoft OneDrive/SharePoint Request Files to receive documents. These services may process technical information or submitted data as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use this website, including page views and completed enquiry journeys. Google Analytics is optional: its tracking script is not loaded unless you choose to accept analytics. If you decline, you can still use the site normally. We do not send the names, email addresses, phone numbers, message contents or uploaded bills from our forms to Google Analytics. Google may set analytics cookies when you accept. You can review how Google processes information in its privacy information.

Checking your analytics choice…

Your analytics choice is saved in your browser and can be changed by selecting Change analytics cookie choice. You can also clear your browser site data to reset this choice. We do not use this GA4 installation for personalised advertising.

Your questions and rights

If you have a question about information you have submitted to Angel Telecom, or want to discuss a data-protection request, please contact us through the online enquiry form.
