PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

How Angel Telecom handles information submitted through this website and how you can contact us about privacy.

Information you choose to send us

If you use our online enquiry form, we may receive your name, company, email address, enquiry details and any file you choose to attach. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and provide the service you have requested.

How we use enquiry information

Information submitted through the website is used for legitimate business purposes connected with your enquiry, such as responding to questions, preparing quotations, discussing mobile or technology requirements, or arranging repair and support services.

Files and mobile bills

If you attach a document, such as a mobile bill or device list, please only include information that is relevant to your enquiry. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information unless we specifically ask for it through an appropriate secure process.

Third-party services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and the online enquiry form is processed by the form service configured on the website. Those services may process technical information or submitted data as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

The website does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics scripts. If this changes, this notice should be updated to explain the relevant services and choices.

Your questions and rights

If you have a question about information you have submitted to Angel Telecom, or want to discuss a data-protection request, please contact us through the online enquiry form.
