WHAT WE REPAIR

Console and controller repair for the faults that stop you gaming.

Availability depends on the console model, fault and parts required, but the repair network supports a wide range of common gaming-device and controller problems.

PlayStation repairs

Supported PlayStation consoles and controllers can be assessed for display, HDMI, power, cooling, storage, analogue-stick and other hardware faults.

Xbox repairs

Xbox consoles and controllers can be assessed for HDMI and display problems, overheating, power faults, disc-drive issues, stick drift and other supported faults.

Nintendo Switch repairs

Supported Nintendo Switch systems and Joy-Con controllers can be assessed for charging, power, screen, analogue-stick drift and other hardware problems.

COMMON CONSOLE FAULTS

Problems we can help diagnose.

If your console or controller is not working properly, tell us what it is doing and we can point you towards the appropriate repair route.

HDMI & no picture

No signal, intermittent display or a damaged HDMI port can stop a console displaying correctly.

Overheating & loud fans

Cooling problems, restricted airflow and dust can cause excessive fan noise, overheating or shutdowns.

Stick drift

Stick drift is a common controller problem where an analogue stick moves on its own or the character/camera moves without the stick being touched. We can assess compatible PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo controllers.

Disc-drive problems

Problems reading, accepting or ejecting discs can be caused by the drive mechanism or related components.

Power faults

We can assess consoles that will not turn on, lose power unexpectedly or show other power-related symptoms.

Storage & software issues

Crashes, failed updates, installation problems and storage-related faults may be diagnosable depending on the system.

CONTROLLER REPAIRS & UPGRADES

Stick drift? Ask about Hall Effect and TMR upgrades.

Stick drift can make a controller frustrating or unusable. For compatible controllers, Pocket Geek Tech Repair can replace traditional analogue stick modules with modern magnetic sensing technology. Hall Effect and TMR options are available only for supported controllers and parts.

Hall EffectMagnetic sensing that avoids the traditional resistive track.
TMRAdvanced magnetic sensing technology for supported controllers.
Model dependentCompatibility and availability are checked before repair.
