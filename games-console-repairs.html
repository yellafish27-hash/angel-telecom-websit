WHAT WE REPAIR

Console repair for the faults that stop you gaming.

Availability depends on the console model, fault and parts required, but the repair network supports a wide range of common gaming-device problems.

PlayStation repairs

Supported PlayStation consoles can be assessed for display, HDMI, power, cooling, storage and other hardware faults.

Xbox repairs

Xbox consoles can be assessed for HDMI and display problems, overheating, power faults, disc-drive issues and other supported faults.

Nintendo Switch repairs

Supported Nintendo Switch systems can be assessed for charging, power, screen, controller and other hardware problems.

COMMON CONSOLE FAULTS

Problems we can help diagnose.

If your console is not working properly, tell us what it is doing and we can point you towards the appropriate repair route.

HDMI & no picture

No signal, intermittent display or a damaged HDMI port can stop a console displaying correctly.

Overheating & loud fans

Cooling problems, restricted airflow and dust can cause excessive fan noise, overheating or shutdowns.

Power faults

We can assess consoles that will not turn on, lose power unexpectedly or show other power-related symptoms.

Disc-drive problems

Problems reading, accepting or ejecting discs can be caused by the drive mechanism or related components.

Storage & software issues

Crashes, failed updates, installation problems and storage-related faults may be diagnosable depending on the system.

Controller problems

Controller faults can include stick drift, connection issues and worn analogue-stick modules.

CONTROLLER REPAIRS & UPGRADES

Stick drift? Ask about Hall Effect and TMR upgrades.

For compatible controllers, Pocket Geek Tech Repair can replace traditional analogue stick modules with modern magnetic sensing technology. Hall Effect and TMR options are available only for supported controllers and parts.

Hall EffectMagnetic sensing that avoids the traditional resistive track.
TMRAdvanced magnetic sensing technology for supported controllers.
Model dependentCompatibility and availability are checked before repair.
