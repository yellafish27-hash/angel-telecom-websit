How a repair enquiry works

Three simple steps.

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. The team confirms the details with you first.

  1. 1

    Choose your store

    Pick the repair centre in Shipley, Halifax, Sheffield or Bolton that suits you.

  2. 2

    Tell us about the repair

    Select your device, brand, model and fault, add your contact details and send the enquiry.

  3. 3

    We confirm the details

    The team gets back to you to confirm price, parts and availability before any repair.
