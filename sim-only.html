BUSINESS SIM ONLY

Keep the phone. Change the SIM.

Modern smartphones can last much longer than a typical two-year contract. If the handset is still right for the user, there may be no reason to replace it, especially when we can repair, refurbish and replace batteries to keep it performing and looking like new.

WHY SIM ONLY?

Stop replacing perfectly good phones just because the contract ends.

SIM only separates the cost of connectivity from the cost of hardware. If a modern handset is still doing its job, you can keep it, repair it and carry on using it rather than automatically replacing it every couple of years.

BUSINESS SIM ONLY CONTRACTS

A flexible way to manage business connectivity.

SIM Only can make sense when your business already has suitable phones, wants to separate connectivity from hardware, or needs a more flexible way to manage different users.

For existing devices

Keep phones that still meet your users' needs and move the connectivity onto a business SIM Only arrangement.

For changing teams

Add or change connections as your business develops without treating every user as though they need a new handset at the same time.

For better cost control

Separate device spending from mobile connectivity so you can review each part of your mobile estate on its own merits.

MAKE DEVICES LAST LONGER

Replace the handset when you need to, not when the contract says you should.

A battery that no longer lasts the day does not necessarily mean the phone is finished. A cracked screen does not necessarily mean it needs replacing. We can repair, refurbish and refresh suitable devices, helping you get more useful life from the technology you already own.
