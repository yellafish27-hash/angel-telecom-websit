WHY SIM ONLY?

Stop replacing perfectly good phones just because the contract ends.

SIM only separates the cost of connectivity from the cost of hardware. If a modern handset is still doing its job, you can keep it, repair it and carry on using it rather than automatically replacing it every couple of years.
