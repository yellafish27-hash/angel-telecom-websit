HYDROGEL SCREEN PROTECTION

Cut specifically for your phone. Fitted by us.

Our hydrogel system lets us cut the protector to the exact phone you have, then fit and finish it professionally.

01

We identify your exact model

We select the correct cutting pattern for your phone from the Devia system, so the protector is made for that specific handset.

02

We cut it while you wait

The plotter cuts the hydrogel film to the required shape, including the relevant screen contours and cut-outs.

03

We fit, seal & finish it

The protector is carefully aligned and applied using the correct installation technique. We finish the edges and treat the film so it can settle properly after fitting.

Why hydrogel? It is a flexible protective film rather than rigid tempered glass, making it useful for curved screens and a wide range of phone shapes. Minor installation marks and bubbles can settle as the film cures.
