Repair support in Shipley.

Shipley is Angel Telecom’s Bradford-area repair location, making it a useful local option for businesses and individuals who want face-to-face access to a repair centre. WHY LOCAL REPAIR MATTERS

Repair, replace or reuse?

If you are managing several business phones, tablets or other devices, we can help you look beyond the immediate repair. Depending on the fault and the device, the sensible route may be a repair, replacement, refurbished handset or recycling. That wider view can help avoid replacing technology unnecessarily.

For specific repair availability, pricing and current store information, use the Pocket Geek Tech Repair store page. For business repair, insurance or device-lifecycle requirements, you can contact Angel Telecom directly.
