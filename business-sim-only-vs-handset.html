Business SIM Only vs Handset Contracts: Which Is Better Value?

The cheapest monthly figure isn't always the cheapest business arrangement.

SIM Only can be attractive when the business already owns reliable phones. A handset-inclusive agreement may suit organisations that need predictable upgrades or new devices. The correct comparison includes the entire contract term.

Calculate the full term

Compare all monthly charges, upfront payments, device financing, extras and any expected repair costs.

Assess current handsets

If devices are secure and working well, retaining them can avoid unnecessary replacement.

Plan for repairs

Screen and battery repairs may be less costly than renewing every handset, depending on the model and condition.

Consider support and control

Check the network, account management, live usage information, spend alerts and help available for changes.

Review individual users

Not every employee needs the same handset, data allowance or renewal cycle.
