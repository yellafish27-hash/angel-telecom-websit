About Angel Telecom

Business mobiles first. Technology support that goes further.

Angel Telecom is an independent Bradford business specialising in business mobiles, SIMs and devices, with repair, refurbishment and technology support alongside what we supply.

Independent by design

A real person, not a call centre.

Angel Telecom has been supplying the needs of businesses and people around Bradford for more than 30 years. The business is built around direct relationships, practical advice and finding a solution that fits the customer rather than forcing everyone into the same model.

Business mobiles sit at the centre of what we do. Around that sits a wider technology lifecycle: devices, repairs, refurbishment, trade-in and recycling.

The approach

Connect. Protect. Repair. Reuse.

  • Straightforward business mobile advice
  • New and refurbished device options
  • Four Pocket Geek repair and service centres
  • Repair support for business and insurance requirements
  • Trade-in and responsible recycling options
